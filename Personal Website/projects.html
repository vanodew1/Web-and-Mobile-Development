Option Pricing: Black-Scholes to Heston

Python · Advanced Differential Equations · April 2026

  • Derived the Black-Scholes PDE from geometric Brownian motion and solved it in closed form using a Green's function.
  • Built a Monte Carlo pricer and a Carr-Madan FFT pricer (4096 strikes in ~12ms), then extended the model to Heston to reproduce the volatility smile.

RISC-V Processor Design

Verilog · Computer Architecture · April 2026

  • Designed a 64-bit, 5-stage pipelined processor with full hazard handling: forwarding, load-use stall detection, and branch resolution with pipeline flush.
  • Synthesized a 32-bit RV32I processor onto a Basys 3 FPGA with memory-mapped I/O and a live 7-segment PC display, verified on hardware with a Fibonacci subroutine. Team of 3.
